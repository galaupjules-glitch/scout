Chrome is blocking your location? Here's how to turn it on:
  1. Click the icon to the left of the site address (the lock or settings icon).
  2. Set Location to Allow, then reload the page.
  3. On a Mac, also check: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Google Chrome turned on.
  4. On a phone: Settings › Chrome › Location › Allow.
